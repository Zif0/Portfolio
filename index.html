About Me

I’ve always preferred a hands-on approach to engineering. My background started on the production floor in quality and manufacturing roles, where I learned how much the boots on the ground details actually matter. Having that foundation gives me a practical perspective on how things are built and how to address the friction that often exists between design and reality.


Right now I am focused on building technical solutions that I can own end to end. I enjoy taking complex, technical environments and building the data solutions required to make them more efficient. I take a lot of pride in being proficient across the stack and ensuring that the systems I build are practical enough to actually work in a real world setting.


In my off hours I am usually still building or optimizing something. That usually looks like pushing the limits of 3D printing with engineering-grade materials, working on mechanical DIY projects, or refining strategy in a competitive game. I am a big believer that being a well-rounded engineer is about staying curious and never really stopping being a builder.
